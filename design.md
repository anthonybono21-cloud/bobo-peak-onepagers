# design.md — Zebra × Peak customer flyer rules

Lives with the flyers. Follow these; don't invent.

## Color

- Core (≥80% of any piece): White `#FFFFFF`, Black `#000000`, Gray 500 `#757575`, Gray 50 `#F2F2F2`.
- Secondary (≤20%): Orange `#FF4D00` (official vibrant accent), Magenta `#FF40FC`, Impact Green `#A8F931`.
- **Anthony override:** the customer-facing accent is **Blue `#1F69FF`**, NOT Impact Green. Impact Green is official/internal only — never the customer accent, never buttons.
- Orange may stay as the $900 punch on the TC78 flyer (his flyer + palette).
- Black background → white headlines and body. Orange text on black is OK for the route line.

## Type

- Headlines: Zebra Sans Condensed Extrabold, sentence case, never all caps.
- Body: Zebra Sans Regular, left aligned, 45–75 characters per line.
- Eyebrows: Zebra Mono Medium, ALL CAPS only.
- If the Zebra otf files are not in the repo, use the official fallbacks ONLY: Arial Narrow Bold / Arial / Trebuchet MS. Name the Zebra faces first in the stack. Never Inter/Geist/Space Grotesk or other invented faces.
- Tracking 0.
- Body ≥10pt print / ≥12pt digital.

## Logo

- Black or white only. Clearspace = height of the letter Z on every side.
- No recolor, no stretch, no rotate. Never on a weak photo. No second extra Zebra logo clutter.
- Co-branded lockup math:
  - `x` = half the Zebra Head width — the space from each mark to the divider.
  - `y` = the ear width — the divider extends `y` above and below the marks.
  - Divider width = `0.5z` (half a face stripe).
  - The partner mark gets the same `x` on the other side.
- Partner mark must work on the page background (white/reversed on black). No invented boxes or tiles behind partner marks.

## Layout

- Print 8.5×11 two-pager. Margins ≥0.25in. Grid.
- Let it breathe — don't pack the front until it chokes. A spec strip can live on the front only if it still breathes; otherwise keep it a quiet bar.
- Buttons, if any: Blue `#1F69FF` fill, white label, pill (Anthony override). No Impact Green buttons.
- No overlapping or stacked text. No stretched photos — crop to panel.

## Photography

- Use the real keyed TC78 photos already in the repo. Do not AI-generate devices. Do not invent lifestyle people.
- Device hero stays, paired renders at equal physical size. Do not stretch.

## Hard fails

- Impact Green as the accent.
- Generic type when you can name the Zebra faces.
- White Peak tile on the black page.
- Invented dollars, invented dates, or invented specs (e.g., a 40-foot scan line).
- FedEx marks or FedEx truck photography.
- Busy brochure density.
